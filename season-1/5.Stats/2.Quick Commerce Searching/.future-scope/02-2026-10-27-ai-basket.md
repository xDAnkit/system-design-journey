# 02. AI basket

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Added | 2026-10-27 |
| Status | Later |
| Effort | Medium |

## What

The shopper types a goal, for example "pav bhaji banani hai". The app shows every ingredient as a real product with one "Add all" button.

## Why

The clearest demo of AI search: it turns an intent into a cart, not just a list of matches.

## How

- Known goals come from `intents.json` first (no LLM call).
- Unknown goals: gemma3:4b returns an ingredient list as JSON (`[{ item, qty }]`).
- Each item runs the normal hybrid search, limited to the shopper's dark store, and takes the top in-stock product.
- Cache the result in Redis by the normalized goal text. The LLM call takes 3 to 4 s, so show a skeleton while it runs.

## Watch out

- The LLM can invent items. Show only items that matched a real product.
- Cap the list at about 12 items.
- Respect a veg / non-veg preference if the shopper set one.
