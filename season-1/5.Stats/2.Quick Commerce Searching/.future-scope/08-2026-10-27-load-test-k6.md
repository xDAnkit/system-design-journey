## What

k6 scripts for `/search` (cold and warm cache), `/search/suggest` and `/events`.

## Why

Real numbers for the video: p50, p95, p99 latency, requests per second, error rate, and what the Redis cache saves.

## How

- Follow the repo's bench rules in `rules/scoped/bench.md` (k6, result columns).
- Compare keyword-only against hybrid search to show the cost of the vector step.
- Run against the test setup (`.env.test`: `blinkit-test` DB, Redis DB 1, `test_` Typesense prefix).

## Watch out

- One server at a time, as the backend rules say. The user starts and stops it.
