# Instagram Story: Architecture Discussion

Working notes for the Insta Story design discussion.

**Goal:** understand how a story works end to end, then design it.

**Scope:** story creation, visibility, author controls, viewer actions, analytics, caching, archival.

---

## 1. How a Story Works

- What is the life of a story? Create, publish, view, expire.
- Who can create a story?
- Default lifetime is 24 hours. What happens at expiry?
- What is the read path: who fetches what, and how fast must it be?

---

## 2. Building Blocks for Story Creation

| Block         | What it covers                                   |
| ------------- | ------------------------------------------------ |
| DB schema     | Story, media, audience, view, interaction tables |
| Media storage | Image and video upload, processing, CDN delivery |
| Endpoints     | Create, read, delete, edit                       |
| Story type    | Public, Followers only, Close friends            |

Questions:

- [ ] Schema shape: one table or separate story and media tables?
- [ ] Media: direct upload to object storage, or through our service?
- [ ] Which endpoints are needed for the first version?
- [ ] Visibility is per story. How is the audience list stored, resolved at write or read?

---

## 3. Extra Features While Creating

- Music
- Tags
- Links
- Questions
- Reactions and polls

Questions:

- [ ] Are these stored with the story or in their own tables?
- [ ] Do interactive items collect a response per viewer? Where does that response live?
- [ ] Do these features change the read payload size?

---

## 4. Author Features

- Delete a story
- Edit a story within 15 minutes
- Highlights

Questions:

- [ ] Edit window of 15 minutes. How is that enforced, by check or by column?
- [ ] Delete: hard delete or soft delete with a tombstone flag?
- [ ] Highlights live beyond 24 hours. Where is that data kept?

---

## 5. Viewer Features

- Views
- Likes
- Replies
- Share

Questions:

- [ ] A view is a write. At story scale, how do we absorb that write load?
- [ ] Is a view counted once per viewer, or once per visit?
- [ ] Likes and replies: synchronous write or queued?

---

## 6. Story Analytics

### 6.1 Audience Actions

- Navigation: next, previous, exit, back
- Profile visits
- Follow

### 6.2 Audience Stats

- Gender
- Country
- Age
- Followers vs non-followers

### 6.3 Clicks

- Links
- Mentions
- Music

### 6.4 Meta One (Creator Tier)

Stats:

- Rewatch count
- Viewer search

Options:

- Extend to 48 hours
- Story spotlight
- Custom friend lists
- Anonymous story preview

Questions:

- [ ] Analytics: computed live from events, or pre-aggregated?
- [ ] Where do we store events before aggregation?
- [ ] Are the same audience stats also used for visibility filtering?

---

## 7. Caching

- Needed for scaling
- Cache the hot reads
- Manage cache in the situations below
- Deletion flags (tombstones)

Questions:

- [ ] What are the hot reads? Story feed, audience list, view count?
- [ ] Which cases must invalidate the cache: new story, delete, edit, expiry?
- [ ] Does a tombstone hide the story immediately on read?
- [ ] Cache TTL vs the 24 hour lifetime. How do the two interact?

---

## 8. Archival

- Older stories move out of the hot path
- Where do archived stories live?
- How does a highlight read archived data?

Questions:

- [ ] Archive trigger: on expiry, or on a scheduled job?
- [ ] After archival, is the story still readable by the author?
- [ ] Do analytics survive archival?

---

## Open Items

- [ ] Decide the first version scope: creation and viewing only, or analytics too?
- [ ] Decide media path: object storage plus CDN.
- [ ] Decide the read path for the story feed.
- [ ] Decide the write path for views.
- [ ] Decide the caching layer and its invalidation rules.
